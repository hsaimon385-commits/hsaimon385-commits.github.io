About Us

FreshBite Café was founded in 2020 with a passion for serving fresh, healthy, and affordable meals. Our story began when our owner, a food enthusiast, wanted to create a cozy spot where locals could enjoy quality food without breaking the bank.

Our Mission: To provide fresh, healthy, and affordable options that nourish the body and bring joy to every meal.
